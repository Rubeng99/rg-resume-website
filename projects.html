ECE 5 team project

During my last quarter at UC San Diego I took a lower division electrical engineering course, ECE 5. I took it because it did not feel right to be in the Jacobs School of Engineering without having taken an ECE course.

We learned to use Onshape to design and print a 3D chassis, and practiced soldering and working with hardware components. I took the initiative of leading a team of four by organizing meeting times to work on the build and fix hardware issues as they came up.

Ultimate Frisbee

During my senior year at UC San Diego I joined the ultimate frisbee team. It was one of the best decisions I made: it let me meet driven people and gave me a way to destress while studying. Ultimate frisbee taught me the importance of communication and working as a team.

See the UC San Diego ultimate frisbee team page.

Long distance running

One of my favorite hobbies is long distance running. As of 2024 I had been running for about ten years. That year I ran the La Jolla Half Marathon in 1 hour 33 minutes, and a month later the Rock 'n' Roll Marathon in 3 hours 50 minutes.

I ran the marathon slower because I over-trained in the days before it and pulled both calf muscles in the last six miles.

Hiking

Another favorite hobby is hiking. What I like most is how grounded in nature it makes me feel.

I joined the hiking club at UC San Diego in 2022, which gave me a better understanding of the world because I met so many people from different places and backgrounds.
